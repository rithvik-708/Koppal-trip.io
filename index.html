Different cities, new places, long walks, road trips, trying new foods, getting lost somewhere and laughing about it later, taking a million pictures, and coming home tired after a beautiful day together. 😍😍😍😍😍😍💖💖💖💖💖

I dream about the ordinary days too. The days when we don't go anywhere, when we're just spending time together, being silly, talking about our day, and finding happiness in the smallest things. I want that kind of love with you, jaanu. The exciting adventures, yes, but also the peaceful, comfortable kind of love where we can simply be ourselves. 🥹🥹🥹🥹🥹🥹💕💕💕💕💕

Because you're not just my baby or my girlfriend, wifey. You're the person I want to keep choosing, keep growing with, and keep making memories with. You're my jaanu, my bacha, my favourite person to annoy, my favourite person to talk to, and the girl who can make daddy smile just by being herself. 😘😘😘😘😘💕💕💕💕💕

So, when we finally meet in Koppal, baby, I want you to forget about making everything perfect. Let's just enjoy ourselves. Let's laugh too loudly, eat good food, take silly pictures, talk endlessly, explore a little, and treasure every moment we get together. 💕💕💕💕💕🥹🥹🥹🥹🥹🥹

Let daddy take care of his baby, let hubby spoil his wifey with love, and let us make a day that we'll remember long after it's over. 😘😘😘😘😘💖💖💖💖💖

And when you read this letter, I hope you can feel even a little bit of the happiness I'm feeling while imagining our time together. I hope you know that somewhere, your daddy is already looking forward to seeing his beautiful girl, holding her hand, and making her smile. 💕💕💕💕💕😘😘😘😘😘

I love you, my wifey. I love you, my jaanu. I love you, my baby, my bacha. 😘😘😘😘😘💖💖💖💖💖

Thank you for being a part of my life, for giving me someone to miss, someone to look forward to seeing, and someone with whom even the simplest day can become a beautiful memory.
